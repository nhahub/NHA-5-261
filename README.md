## 🌟 Quick Overview

**CityVision** is an integrated urban intelligence platform standardizing and analyzing multi-domain data across **5 key Egyptian Governorates**. The system evaluates live/historical urban performance and establishes the data foundation for future **Predictive Time-Series Analytics**.

---

## 🏛️ Governorates Coverage

<div align="center">

| 📍 Governorate / City | 🎯 Focus & Urban Domain Profiling |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🏰 **Cairo (القاهرة)** | High-density traffic bottlenecks, transit load, and peak air emissions |
| 🌊 **Alexandria (الإسكندرية)** | Weather fluctuations, coastal transit disruption & traffic impact |
| 🌾 **Tanta (طنطا)** | Agricultural-urban transit connections, central Delta movement & grid energy |
| ⚓ **Suez (السويس)** | Industrial energy utilization, canal corridor traffic & accident risk |
| 🏖️ **Sharm El-Sheikh (شرم الشيخ)** | Tourism energy demand shifts, seasonal weather & air quality stability |
